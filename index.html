点测及缺失值 / Spot readings and missing values：只有点测 RI 的材料按各独立读数及原表 Range 匹配，不连成区间。原表未提供 RI 数值的材料不因 RI 被排除，并明确标为未知，不作“任意值”解释。 / Spot-only RI entries use separate readings plus source Range. Materials without numeric source RI remain candidates, explicitly labelled unknown rather than Any.

筛选 / Filtering：RI、SG 先计入原表 Range，再叠加独立的测量误差 ±。颜色包括常见色、少见色及修饰色；保留原表 P（Purple，紫）与 V（Violet，蓝紫）的区分。选择 ADR 时按 SR 检索；AGG 同时参考光性栏及备注。拼合石原表为 Any，不因数值或光性被排除。 / Source variation is applied before the independent measurement tolerance. All chart colour categories are searchable. Selecting ADR searches SR materials. Additional AGG reactions come from comments. Assembled stones remain possible for any readings.

光谱星号 / Spectrum key：*** 几乎总能发现；** 较少发现；两者出现时须结合其他测试。* 有时发现，不一定具诊断性。空白表示未发现或不具诊断性。 / *** Almost always found; ** less often found; either supports identification when combined with other tests. * Sometimes found, not always diagnostic. Blank: none found or not diagnostic.
